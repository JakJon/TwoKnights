# Idea backlog

Raw ideas from playtest notes, kept so they don't get lost. Nothing here is
committed or designed — these are prompts for a later pass, not a plan.

Captured 2026-08-19 from testing notes.

## Upgrades

- **Magic bow** — the next X shots from the bow home on the nearest enemy and
  deal X damage.
- **Ice tip** — slows enemies; stacks, and enough stacks freeze one outright.

## Specials

- Temporary invincibility.
- Recover health per kill.
- **Giant's feet** — stomps enemies for you.
- Special bar fill rate: fill the meter faster.

## Enemies

- **Healer** — does nothing but heal other enemies.
- **Tiny enemies** — hard to hit, small damage.
- **Kobra** — inflicts poison.
- **Lasers** — a beam down one direction that must be blocked by the shield.

## Challenges

- Kill the rat knight with no upgrades.
- Kill a giant red slime with the sword.
- Shoot a non-ignited arrow through a fire cloud AND a poison cloud, and land
  both statuses on one enemy.
- Kill 2 (maybe 3) enemies at once with an explosive mine cart.

## Power-downs

- **Concussed** — shield orbit radius extended.
- **Blinded** — part of the screen is covered.
- **Poison** — 1 damage every 5 seconds, in varying strengths.

## Misc

- **Three blind mice** — rare pattern: three mice in dark glasses wandering
  aimlessly.
- A deflectable projectile that has to be bounced into something.
